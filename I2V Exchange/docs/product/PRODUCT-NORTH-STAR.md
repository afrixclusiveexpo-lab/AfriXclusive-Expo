# Product North Star

## The product in one sentence
A living innovation network where university students and early-stage builders **show real work, gain credibility through documented progress, compete, pitch, collaborate and connect with people who can help bring it to life**.

## Not another directory, not an exchange
- Not a generic startup link list: each page is a **project that evolves** through milestones, authentic media, events and connections.
- Not a substitute for VC4A, F6S, Devpost, incubators, university technology transfer or Zimbabwe's securities exchanges; it connects earlier-stage creators to the appropriate opportunities. Existing products serve some of these jobs; research before asserting a unique advantage.
- Not a broker, securities venue, trustee, KYC provider or legal arbiter without relevant legal structure and permits.

## Value hierarchy
1. **See it:** a compelling real demo/prototype and concise explanation.
2. **Understand it:** what problem is solved, stage, evidence, team and needs.
3. **Trust its status:** who claims what, which credentials/outcomes are verified, what remains unproven.
4. **Act:** follow, contact, join, mentor, invite to a demo day, sponsor, challenge or pilot.
5. **Keep the story:** the same project's milestones/competitions/pitches/outcomes accumulate over time.

## Seven principal jobs
- **Student innovator:** present class/research work without needing company registration; control sensitive details; find support.
- **Startup team:** document traction, manage introductions, prepare pitches, submit once to many appropriate programmes.
- **Public visitor/alumnus:** discover authentic projects and follow their progress without a forced identity-document gate.
- **University organizer:** publish approved showcases, run fair competitions and measure documented outcomes with permissions.
- **Judge/mentor:** see a relevant, permissioned briefing; evaluate using clear rubrics; give useful feedback.
- **Sponsor/corporate scout:** present real challenges; identify and contact technically relevant project teams; agree terms before accessing submissions.
- **Investor/accelerator:** discover credible emerging companies and request lawful, controlled due diligence.

## Five recurring product loops
1. **Showcase loop:** create -> publish -> discover -> engage -> improve profile.
2. **Progress loop:** set milestone -> attach evidence -> update -> followers learn -> new opportunity.
3. **Competition loop:** enter once -> judge -> pitch -> receive feedback -> retain achievement -> continue building.
4. **Challenge loop:** sponsor specifies problem/terms -> teams propose -> evaluate -> pilot or award -> evidence added to project.
5. **Institutional loop:** curate students -> host event -> discover outcomes -> strengthen partnership -> invite next cohort.

## Distinctive product propositions to test, not market as proven
- A permanent project passport across competitions, pitches and collaborations.
- A visual, student-friendly profile that presents real work beautifully.
- Campus-centric discovery, demo days and sponsor challenges in one system.
- Transparent claim/evidence badges instead of fabricated 'AI market caps'.
- Low-bandwidth pitch participation with slide/audio fallback.

## Accessibility and inclusion
Public viewing without mandatory KYC; clear language; keyboard navigation; transcripts/captions where feasible; readable on smaller phones; scalable images/video; student privacy; appropriate handling of minors if accepted. English-first may be practical for a pilot, but avoid making multilingual support structurally impossible.

## Measurable outcomes (pilot hypotheses; not promises)
- Qualified creator activation: % of onboarded creators who publish a useful project with consent.
- Meaningful engagement: number of approved mentor/demo/partnership requests, not likes.
- Submission completion and judging turnaround; evidence of fairness/disputes resolved.
- Project development: percentage of projects with meaningful milestones or external outcomes.
- Institution retention: continued event hosting and outcomes for active partners.
- User trust: unauthorized-access incidents, abuse-report handling and informed disclosure behavior.

## Content precedence
Confirmed founder intent in `PROJECT_STATE.md` > approved current PRD > implementation decisions/ADRs > provisional research > historical blueprint archive. Keep dated decisions and mark changes rather than quietly editing out disagreements.
