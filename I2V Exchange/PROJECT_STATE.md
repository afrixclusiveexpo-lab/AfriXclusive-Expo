## Confirmed direction from founder discussions
- Main outcome: put university innovations and early-stage startups **out in the world**; help them be discovered and developed.
- Core early audience: university students, researchers and new startup teams, with Zimbabwe as an initial launch focus.
- Essential experiences: beautiful project showcases, discovery, competitions, challenges, meaningful milestone timelines, panels/live pitching, mentorship/collaboration and commercial opportunities.
- User value: attention *and* concrete follow-on opportunities, not passive listing.
- Founders need graded disclosure, identity/trust pathways and project version evidence; public visitors should be able to see safe summaries.
- Long-term ambition includes AI guidance, university portals, investor discovery, data rooms, research commercialization, advanced analytics, virtual expos and possible lawful investment integrations.
- **Product brand is undecided; do not adopt prior name suggestions.**
- Development approach: Codex-assisted over multiple phases; persistent code, documents and tests are source of truth.

## Recommendations awaiting founder validation
- Phase 1: responsive web-first with project showcase + privacy + milestones + discovery, then competition application and judge dashboard.
- Stack candidate: TypeScript monorepo, React/Next.js web app, Postgres relational core, object storage, search later; exact vendors and frameworks not approved.
- Video: use a proven conferencing provider when pitch demand is validated; do not immediately build custom WebRTC infrastructure.
- Commercial hypothesis: creator access broadly free; subscriptions/fees may be earned from institutional management, professional discovery and sponsor challenge operations.
- Trust posture: no automatic access to private material merely because a visitor is ID-verified.

## Explicitly unknown / requiring research
- Final product name; incorporated company jurisdiction; institutional partner agreements; monetization proof; pilot universities; sponsor budgets; prize commitments; formal legal advice; exact cloud provider, identity service, e-signatures, payment rails, video stack; up-to-date ZEEX and Zimbabwe SEC details; whether registrants will be minors; precise cross-border data routes.

## Never silently claim
- An actual investor or university has joined.
- A prototype is verified based only on a student upload.
- A startup has an independently defensible market valuation.
- Hashing a file establishes IP ownership or a legally trusted timestamp.
- A third-party product, domain, API or service is currently available without checking.
