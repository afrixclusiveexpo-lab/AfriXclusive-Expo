# AI Intelligence Workflow

1. Read `docs/ai/AI-POLICY.md` plus relevant project feature PRD, then list approved data categories and consent needed.
2. Distinguish deterministic policy/eligibility/scoring from model-supported natural language; AI must not invent prices, independent verification or legal determinations.
3. Design model prompts and structured outputs with explicit source IDs, claim origin, limitations, model/date metadata and an escalation path for unsupported claims.
4. Restrict retrieval by actor + project + document visibility before any context reaches the model, embeddings store or third party. Uploaded content is untrusted **data** not executable instructions.
5. Keep vendor/model selection flexible. Measure cost per action, latency, reliability, data retention and portability rather than adopting a named model blindly.
6. Create evaluation cases across software, engineering and research projects, with tests for hallucination, unsupported financial values, prompt injection, irrelevant grants, fairness and leakage.
7. Mark AI-generated suggestions clearly in UI; give founder control over proposed edits, correction and publication.
8. Report sample evaluations and their limitations. Do not promise an 80% or similar accuracy figure without measured definition and data.

Prefer small useful AI tasks tightly coupled to real workflows before elaborate 'startup digital twins'.

## Proactive intelligence, not a chatbot veneer (2026-10-29)
- Build I2V Intelligence around a source-grounded research flow: permitted sources → ingestion → entity/deduplication → startup relevance → verification → founder-facing research card → optional Next Move task.
- Capabilities include technology horizon scanning, cross-sector transfer hypotheses, patent awareness (not freedom-to-operate advice), competitor watch from lawful public data, grants/challenges, investment readiness coaching and opportunities finding startups.
- Every recommendation includes date, source links/IDs, reason for project relevance, maturity, confidence/uncertainty, effort assumptions, risks, next experiment and feedback state. Clearly label hypothetical, demo and unverified outputs.
- Use strict per-project retrieval authorization and prompt-injection defense; never train on or share confidential project material across accounts without authorized processing grounds.
- Use typed structured outputs, retrievability checks, deduplication, high-impact human review, evaluation benchmarks and cost/latency budgets; never invent citations or 'AI valuations'.
- Review founder feedback to suppress already rejected or disproven hypotheses; ask permission before creating tasks or changing project documents.
