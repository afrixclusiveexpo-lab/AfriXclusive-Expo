# High-Fidelity Experience Storyboard (for Design and Frontend Agents)

This is a narrative interface brief, not finished artwork. All projects/events here are explicitly **fictional demonstrations**.

## Discovery — 10-second proof
A visitor on a 360px phone sees: strong editorial headline **"Discover what students are building."** and an image slot for a real mechanical prototype; below it, creator-stage badges and clear 'Needs: testing partner'. A card labeled *Solar Coldchain — fictional demo* displays a photo placeholder, prototype status (founder-reported), sector, and 'View build'. Filters by university, sector and stage are available without requiring account registration. No price chart or invented VC amount.

## Project — 60-second understanding
Project detail opens with legible 2-line purpose, a demo gallery, problem/solution pair and **"How you can help"** callout. Milestones show one dated 'initial test' explicitly founder-claimed; technical drawings remain private. A sticky mobile-safe action **"Request a demo"** routes to a short account creation/login and consented contact form.

## Founder workspace — from idea to public page
Creator sees a private draft and a 4-step builder: Story -> Demonstration -> Team and needs -> Privacy review. Uploading 'research-notes.pdf' defaults to private and a lock. Publication preview says: **"These are the details strangers can see."** User confirms before public indexing. After publish, copy share link and 'Add first milestone'.

## Competition — excitement with information
Page hero uses authentic event media or a neutral illustration placeholder, not fabricated partner branding. Date and applicant timezone visible. Tabs: Overview, Eligibility, How it works, Evaluation, Prizes and terms. CTA **"Enter with a project"** leads to approved project snapshots, not a fresh company profile. A progress stepper shows Submitted -> Eligibility review -> Judging -> Live final -> Results.

## Judge console — precise, calm and fair
Judge sees an assigned project and immutable rules version; each criterion shows scoring anchors. If they declare a conflict, score controls lock with clear next steps. Submitted score yields a receipt and no visibility of other judges' private opinions unless event rules authorize it.

## Challenge — trust before urgency
Corporate problem with measurable success criteria, sponsor verification status, prize/pilot terms and prominent **"Who owns your work?"** disclosure. Submission is private by default. CTA **"Review terms and apply"** rather than 'Get rich'.

## Pitch stage — performance with control
Large deck/video view, visible speaker and synchronized timer, modest panel portraits and a moderated Q&A queue. Stage top banner **"Private panel — invited participants only"** or **"Public event"**; a separate recording lamp OFF by default. Bad connectivity triggers **"Switch to audio + slides"**. A post-pitch card summarizes judges' *actual submitted* feedback and accepted follow-up interest.

## One project, one continuing identity
If the fictional Solar Coldchain project moves through prototype milestone -> university contest -> pitch session -> sponsor challenge finalist, the same project URL retains its story. The project never presents an unsupported dollar figure merely because it receives attention.

## Copy micro-patterns
- Good: "Research prototype · Founder reported", "Institution not yet verified", "Only these judges can read your submission", "Recording is off", "Award announced; fulfillment pending".
- Avoid: "Guaranteed patent protection", "Officially worth $1.2M", "The best innovator in Zimbabwe" without verifiable award/context, "University verified" without authorized verification.

## Design handoff checklist
Realistic hierarchy; media fallbacks; disclosure chips; long titles; role-specific components; keyboard path; 360/768/1280px mock states; reflow under 200% zoom; reduced-motion; low-data; errors; timer accuracy; permissions; screen reader labels.
