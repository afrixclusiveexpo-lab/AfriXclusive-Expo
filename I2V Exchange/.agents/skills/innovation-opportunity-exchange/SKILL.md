# Reverse Opportunity Exchange Workflow

1. Determine the *actual participant action*: qualified intro, pilot application, lab booking request, mentor slot, technical collaboration or licensing inquiry; never disguise an unregulated investment transaction.
2. Model project capabilities, needs, sector, stage, location/preferences and sharing permissions independently from public investor relations claims.
3. Rank opportunities with explainable evidence: eligibility gate first, then ability match, capacity, timing, geography, conflicts and stated interest. Include 'why matched' and 'why might not fit'.
4. For corporate problem statements and secret briefs, match on expressly approved attributes; never expose protected corporate or startup information through notifications or embeddings.
5. Build dual-sided workflows: organizer verification → scoped challenge → suggested candidates → consented founder opt-in → intro request → review → meeting → outcomes.
6. Model pilots with objectives, parties, evaluation metrics, data/IP terms, consent, follow-up and completion evidence. Lab and manufacturing requests require real capacity and provider confirmation before 'booked'.
7. Track qualified introductions and realized outcomes; prevent spam/unsolicited contact. Users may mute, opt out, correct eligibility and report abuse.
8. Test bad matches, conflicts, revoked access, sponsor impersonation, closed opportunities and repeated notification storms.

Definition of done: clear opt-in matching and one verified-outcome workflow, with demo labels, permissions and real-provider gaps tracked.
