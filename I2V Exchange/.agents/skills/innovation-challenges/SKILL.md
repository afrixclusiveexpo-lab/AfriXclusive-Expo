# Sponsor Challenge Workflow

1. Read `docs/product/CHALLENGES-PRD.md` and `docs/security/TRUST-MODEL.md`.
2. Separate a public challenge description from confidential sponsor data and **private entrant proposals**. Sponsors do not automatically own IP through platform submission.
3. Design organizer verification, challenge publication with rule/IP terms, eligibility, Q&A, team collaboration, entry receipt, judging, decisions and optional pilot follow-through.
4. Store the exact **terms version agreed by each entrant**; material later changes require re-consent, not silent updates.
5. Differentiate prize award, paid pilot, technology license, procurement and equity investment as different types of arrangements; do not implement unapproved money or title transfers.
6. Protect teams from fake sponsors, hidden IP transfer and prize non-delivery; include reporting and escalation.
7. Build the smallest viable challenge administration workflow first; add structured assessment, live pitch and agreement connectors after demonstrated need.
8. Test deny-by-default proposals, sponsor permissions, deadline treatment, term versioning, award evidence and audit trails.

Report which challenges are fictitious seed data; don't claim any manufacturer has joined.

## Corporate challenges → commercial outcomes (2026-10-29)
- Support problem statements from companies, campuses and institutions; challenge type: competition, research request, paid pilot, sponsored development, procurement discovery or open innovation.
- Challenges must disclose eligibility, scoring, award conditions, data visibility, governing legal terms, IP ownership and permitted sponsor use *before* submission.
- Keep paid contracts, grants, donations, licensing, loans and share offerings distinct. No automatic assignment of participants' invention rights.
- Build consented submission, conflict disclosures, secure organizer review, appeals and permissions-conscious company-project matching.
- Track impact: relevant applications, shortlist conversion, qualified meetings, executed pilot agreements and confirmed outcomes, not only page views.
- Add verifiable sponsor profiles and anti-fraud reporting before a live challenge can collect real-world submissions.
