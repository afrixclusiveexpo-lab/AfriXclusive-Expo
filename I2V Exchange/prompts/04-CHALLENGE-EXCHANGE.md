Sponsor identity status -> drafted real-world problem -> organizer publishes versioned terms/IP conditions and evaluation rubric -> student submits proposal (private by default) -> reviewer scores only assigned submissions -> applicant receives result -> permitted follow-up pilot/introduction can be requested.

Make terms impossible to miss. Do not transfer IP automatically or imply a sponsor has signed a contract.
