# Research Protocol — What Codex May Treat as Fact

## Why
Platform strategy depends on live Zimbabwean university programmes, ZEEX/capital market developments, privacy rules, events, prizes, domain/service prices and available third-party APIs. Earlier compiled research is **valuable background, not automatically current truth**.

## Source quality and claims
- **Founder-established:** explicit product intent in `PROJECT_STATE.md`.
- **Verified current external fact:** sourced to named source, URL, capture/access date, publication date and scope; prefer university sites, SEC Zimbabwe, official gov, official vendor docs.
- **Third-party report:** attribute to named outlet and distinguish claims vs verified legal status.
- **Hypothesis:** competitive gap, willingness to pay, anticipated adoption, expected market value; needs tests.
- **Fictional demo content:** labeled prominently; never launder into actual market facts.

## Prior research file caveat
`knowledge/prior-blueprints/` preserves prior reports and 21-page blueprint; some source citations are not machine-readable citations and some findings may age. Check published primary sources before relying on regulatory, pricing, market, partner or platform status claims.

## Current verification checklist
- Zimbabwe entrepreneurship exchange (ZEEX): precise legal entity, services available **now**, SEC sandbox vs fully approved, whether student ventures are eligible, partner referral rules.
- University innovation hubs: active programmes, authority to syndicate projects, ownership/credit policies and university approvals.
- Competitors VC4A/F6S/Devpost/HeroX/Product Hunt and regional startups; identify actual feature gaps, no 'first ever' claims.
- Zimbabwe Data Protection Act, regulator updates, cross-border storage, student/minor rights; counsel validation.
- WIPO/IP basics for patentable disclosure, assignments, licensing and university ownership.
- Vendor terms, pricing, data residency for conferences/KYC/e-sign/mail/AI/cloud.

## Research deliverable template
Question -> context/why it changes decision -> primary links -> dates -> verified findings -> interpretation -> uncertainty -> design implication -> recommended validation step. Mark inaccessible links and stale sources; do not invent confirmation.

## Competitor differentiation principle
Design for **early-stage university creators with persistent evidence and meaningful interaction across events**. Confirm that the relevant competitor doesn't already offer the exact feature before advertising it as unique. Competitive strength may come from integration, quality, trust and university distribution—not novelty of each individual feature.
