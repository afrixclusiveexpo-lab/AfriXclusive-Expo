# Startup Graduation and Alumni Workflow

1. Read the founder decisions, permanent project identity rules, and current showcase schemas.
2. Support three distinct pathways: **independent venture**, **licensed/commercialized technology**, and **validated research impact**; do not force every useful invention to become a startup.
3. Use transparent, sector-appropriate graduation criteria (product validation, responsible governance, finances where relevant, operational independence, outcomes). Never automatically graduate at an arbitrary AI score.
4. Separate student-entered evidence, third-party verified milestones, decision-maker approvals and official securities-exchange admission. I2V graduation does **not** imply listing eligibility.
5. Preserve project history on graduation and move display classification to alumni rather than deleting a startup. Founder can correct or dispute records; comply with deletion/privacy requirements.
6. Track consent-based 3-, 6-, 12-, 24-, 36-month and optional annual follow-ups with observation timestamps, verification provenance, public/private separation and no fabricated success claims.
7. Support alumni mentor/judge/sponsor participation without granting hidden access to founders' confidential information.
8. Test closed/failed/paused/pivoted businesses, revoked credentials, revoked consent, project mergers, shared ownership and false graduation claims.

Definition of done: a complete graduation review and public/private alumni journey with permissions, time-based outcomes and honest reporting.
