## Working research governance
- Annotate externally asserted facts with source ID, URL, publication date, retrieval date and a confidence/verification level.
- Never present marketing claims, startup counts or programme availability as verified operational outcomes without corroboration.
- Recheck ZEEX's active product status, regulators, rates, programme conditions and institution policy before any commercial/legal launch.
- Separate `documented existing` from `planned idea`, `proposed differentiator`, `hypothesis needing interviews`, `regulatory approval required`.
- The core document is strategic, not a substitute for Zimbabwean securities, privacy and IP legal advice.
