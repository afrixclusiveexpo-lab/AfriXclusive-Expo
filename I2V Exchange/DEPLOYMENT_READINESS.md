# Deployment Readiness and Production Gates

This is a starter specification, not a release approval.

Gates: threat model; abuse/moderation plan; tenant boundaries; authentication; privacy/consent; confidential documents; backups/restore; auditability; accessibility; low-bandwidth checks; dependency/SBOM review; testing; incident response; legal approvals; real services; monitoring; rollback; human production signoff.
